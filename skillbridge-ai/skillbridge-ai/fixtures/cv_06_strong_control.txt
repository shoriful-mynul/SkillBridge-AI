PROJECTS
Urdu Speech Keyword Spotting (final year project, 2 students)
Trained a small CNN to detect 12 Urdu command words on-device. Public Urdu
speech data was scarce, so we recorded 3,100 utterances from 47 speakers across
two cities to get accent spread. Baseline MFCC + CNN reached 91% on our held-out
speakers but only 74% on a third city we had not recorded, which told us we were
fitting accent rather than phoneme. Added SpecAugment and speaker-disjoint
cross-validation; held-out-city accuracy reached 86%. Quantised to int8 for a
Cortex-M4, 41ms inference, 180KB.
Code: github.com/example-zq/urdu-kws

Load Forecasting for a Campus Microgrid
Compared ARIMA, gradient boosting, and an LSTM on 2 years of 15-minute campus
load data. Gradient boosting won (MAPE 4.2% vs LSTM 4.9%) and trained in a
fraction of the time, so we shipped that. Wrote up why the LSTM underperformed:
with 70k samples and strong daily seasonality, the tree model with lag features
had the better inductive bias.
