I would count missing ages by column with df.isna().sum(), split into train and held-out data first, and fit a median imputer only on training ages. I would reuse that imputer to transform held-out rows. I choose median rather than mean because extreme ages can move the mean; however median filling can hide missingness patterns. If a training column is entirely empty, I would flag it and use a documented fixed fallback rather than learn from the held-out rows. For the project manager: keep the check independent, like marking an exam with an answer key that was never used to teach the test answers.
